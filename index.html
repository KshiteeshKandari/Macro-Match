How to play

More / Less: two foods, numbers hidden. Click the one you think has more. One miss and it's over. Each correct pick scores 100–200 (more for close calls), and your streak multiplies it up to ×2.

Guess the Value: type your best guess for 5 foods. Points curve with accuracy: 10% off earns about 810, 50% off only 250. Within 15% adds a bonus, within 5% a bigger one, and an exact guess +500. Stay within 20% on back-to-back foods for a ×1.25, then ×1.5 hot streak.

Cuisines: pick a cuisine on the setup screen to play with whole dishes. Tap the i on a high fat, carb, sodium or sugar dish for lighter ways to cook it.

All numbers are for a typical serving, shown on every card.
